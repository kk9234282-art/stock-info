# Port editorial illustration

`port-editorial.webp` is a static generated illustration created for this prototype and compressed to WebP for the website. It depicts generic, fictional port and logistics infrastructure. It is not a verified photograph of an APSEZ asset and must not be attributed to Adani Ports or used as evidence of its operating footprint.

The gallery caption and alternative text label the imagery as illustrative. The reference screenshots guide page layout only; their photographs, citation badges and numerical financial claims were not copied into the research database.

Replace the illustration with actual company photographs only after verifying the image, rights and required attribution. This asset adds no AI service or image-generation functionality to the website.
